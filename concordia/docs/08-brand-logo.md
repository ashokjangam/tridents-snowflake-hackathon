# Brand and logo brief

## Brand

- Name: **Concordia**
- Tagline: **Different Teams. Same Truth.**
- Secondary line: **Ask naturally. Resolve consistently.**

Meaning: concord, agreement, and shared understanding across teams that begin
with different systems and terminology.

The name is locked for the hackathon. It is not trademark-cleared for
commercial release.

## Brand idea

Many operational paths resolve into one governed line.

The identity should communicate:

- convergence without forced sameness;
- traceability;
- precision;
- calm enterprise confidence;
- evidence over hype.

Avoid:

- chain-link clichés;
- globes;
- trucks, boxes, factories, handshakes;
- generic network-node constellations;
- infinity symbols;
- blockchain styling;
- AI sparkles/brains/robots;
- gradients and neon cyber aesthetics.

## Visual direction

Primary concept: a wordmark plus a compact “convergence ledger” mark.

The mark uses three distinct horizontal paths representing planning,
procurement, and logistics. They approach from different positions, pass through
a disciplined alignment field, and resolve onto one baseline. The paths remain
visibly separate before convergence so the symbol does not imply erased
perspectives.

Style:

- geometric but not cold;
- low-detail;
- legible at 16px;
- works in one colour;
- suitable for a Streamlit sidebar, presentation title, favicon, and Snowflake
  object icon.

## Palette

- Ink navy: authority and structure.
- Warm ivory: approachable working canvas.
- Governed teal: verified definition/evidence.
- Muted copper: exception or changed assumption.
- Slate: metadata.

Exact accessible tokens should be chosen during UI implementation and validated
for WCAG contrast. The logo must work without colour.

## Typography

Wordmark:

- custom or carefully modified contemporary serif/sans hybrid;
- generous spacing;
- no futuristic stencil styling.

Interface:

- accessible humanist sans;
- tabular numerals for metrics.

## Master image-generation prompt

Create a distinctive professional logo system for an enterprise supply-chain
semantic intelligence product named “Concordia”, with the tagline “Different
Teams. Same Truth.”

Concept: three independent operational paths represent planning, procurement,
and logistics. They begin separately and converge through a precise alignment
structure onto one shared baseline, symbolizing different teams resolving to
one governed metric definition. The paths must remain visibly distinct before
convergence. Design a proprietary abstract “convergence ledger” mark, not a
literal chain, truck, box, factory, globe, handshake, infinity loop, database
cylinder, network-node constellation, AI brain, sparkle, or blockchain icon.

Style: calm, exact, high-trust enterprise design; intelligent but understated;
geometric with subtle human warmth; flat vector construction; balanced negative
space; strong silhouette; legible as a 16px favicon and as a presentation
wordmark. No gradients, shadows, 3D, mockups, photographic textures, neon,
rainbow palettes, or generic tech clichés.

Deliverables in one identity sheet:
1. primary horizontal wordmark with mark;
2. compact standalone mark;
3. monochrome black and white versions;
4. dark-background reversed version;
5. small-size/favicon test;
6. clear-space and minimum-size guide.

Suggested palette: deep ink navy, warm ivory, governed teal, muted copper, and
slate, while ensuring the mark works perfectly in one colour. Use a refined
custom wordmark for CONCORDIA and keep the tagline secondary, highly legible,
and free of decorative effects.

The final identity should feel appropriate beside Snowflake, SAP, Palantir,
ServiceNow, and modern enterprise analytics products while remaining original
and recognisable.

## Negative prompt

No chain links, logistics arrows circling a globe, trucks, warehouses, shipping
containers, cubes, generic flowchart nodes, neural networks, brains, robot
heads, magic wands, stars/sparkles, shields, checkmark-in-circle logos, leaf
icons, infinity marks, blockchain hexagons, gradients, glassmorphism, 3D
extrusion, glossy mockups, stock-logo appearance, illegible micro-detail, or
similarity to existing enterprise marks.

## UI usage

- Sidebar: compact mark plus Concordia.
- Header: horizontal wordmark; tagline only on landing/about.
- Metric/evidence screens: no decorative logo repetition.
- Simulation disclosure must never be hidden by brand styling.
